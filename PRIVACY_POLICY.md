# Privacy Policy for the Email Messages (IMAP) TRMNL Plugin

## What the plugin reads

On every refresh, the plugin reads the subject, sender, date and read/flag state of your latest messages from your mail server over IMAP. It does not read message bodies or attachments.

## Where that happens

Inside TRMNL's own serverless environment. The plugin's author runs no server for it: your email address, app password and mail never reach the author.

## What is stored

- Your settings (including the app password) are stored by TRMNL, as for any plugin.
- The last result is kept by TRMNL to show on your screen and to fall back on when your mail server is briefly unreachable.
- Nothing is stored by the plugin's author.

See TRMNL's own privacy policy for how TRMNL handles plugin data.
